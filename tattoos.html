Each Norse symbol carries a story. Use this library to find symbols that match your intentions—protection, strength, guidance, or transformation.

Valknut

Associated with Odin, warriors, and the passage between life and death.

Vegvísir

A guidance symbol, often interpreted as “that which shows the way.”
